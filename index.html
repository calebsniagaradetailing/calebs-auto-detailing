Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer mobile detailing?

Yes. Mobile detailing is available in Lockport, NY and surrounding areas. Call for availability at your location.

How much does detailing cost?

Starting prices range from $50+ to $190+ for the listed interior packages. Final pricing depends on vehicle size and condition.

Why are prices starting prices?

Vehicle size, condition, buildup, and the requested service can change the time and materials needed, so final pricing is confirmed after discussing the vehicle.

How long does a detail take?

Time depends on the service, vehicle size, and condition. A time estimate can be discussed when you call for your quote.

Can every stain be removed?

Not every stain can be completely removed. Results depend on the stain, fabric, age, and previous treatments.

Can scratches be removed?

Paint enhancement can improve gloss and reduce the appearance of some defects, but complete scratch removal is not guaranteed.

Do you offer ceramic coatings?

Yes. Ceramic protection options are available upon request, including 1-year, 3-year, 5-year, and 8-year options.

How long do ceramic coatings last?

Longevity depends on the coating, preparation, maintenance, environment, and vehicle care. Longer-term options may need to be ordered before the appointment.

Do I need an appointment?

Please call ahead so availability and the right service can be discussed before you arrive.
